REPRODUCIBLE DELIVERY

CRISP-DM lifecycle

01

Business understanding

Teach decoder-only LM fundamentals under laptop constraints; success means reproducible loss reduction and working inference.

02

Data understanding

Inspect a repository-owned dialogue corpus, UTF-8 byte coverage, train/validation sizes, and representativeness limits.

03

Data preparation

Fit a local word/punctuation vocabulary, split complete dialogues at 85/15, and sample next-token windows without external downloads.

04

Modeling

Train a pre-norm causal Transformer with RoPE, SwiGLU, RMSNorm, tied embeddings, AdamW, and cosine decay.

05

Evaluation

Track held-out loss and perplexity; optional bounded search compares capacity under the same short training budget.

06

Deployment

Persist config + weights, load lazily in Flask, expose health/metrics/chat APIs, and monitor artifacts here.

Built for transparent, laptop-scale language-model learning.

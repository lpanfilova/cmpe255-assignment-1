END-TO-END METHOD

CRISP-DM, adapted for temporal decisions.

Each phase has an output, decision gate, and feedback path. Forecasting is iterative: deployment monitoring feeds the next business-understanding cycle.

01

Business understanding

Question: What monthly capacity should planners prepare for over the next year?

Success: holdout MASE below 1, low bias, explainable seasonal shape, and reproducible generation.

Risks: structural breaks, external shocks, and confusing correlation with causation.

Decision brief
02

Data understanding

Profile 144 monthly observations from Jan 1949–Dec 1960. Verify the time grain, contiguous index, trend, increasing seasonal amplitude, peaks, and troughs.

Gate: zero missing, duplicate, or non-positive values; monthly frequency confirmed.

Quality report
03

Data preparation

Sort chronologically, preserve the untouched final 24 months, and create deterministic calendar/trend features only from timestamps. Use multiplicative seasonality where amplitude scales with level.

Leakage control: all estimators fit only on pre-holdout history.

Model-ready series
04

Modeling

Benchmark seasonal naïve, Holt-Winters additive trend with multiplicative seasonality, and Ridge regression on log demand with polynomial trend and calendar harmonics/dummies.

Why three: baseline, classical statistical model, and regularized feature model provide useful diversity without excessive compute.

Candidate forecasts
05

Evaluation

Rank on holdout MASE, then inspect MAE, RMSE, MAPE, and bias. Sanity-check future non-negativity and seasonal shape. The champion is {{ results.champion }}.

Limit: one terminal holdout is transparent but does not replace rolling-origin validation for high-stakes use.

Champion decision
06

Deployment

Write a versionable JSON evidence contract, expose read-only forecast and health endpoints, and render decision, model, methodology, and governance views from the same artifact.

Monitor: refresh monthly; alert at MASE > 1 or materially biased errors.

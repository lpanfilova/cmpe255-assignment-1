06 · Deployment & synthesis

Triangulate; do not flatten distinct questions

Supervised models best address prediction. Clusters summarize recurring profiles. Isolation Forest surfaces cases for review. Rules express co-occurrence. LSH retrieves similar profiles efficiently. Agreement across methods strengthens descriptive confidence, but none establishes causality. Deployment here means a versioned JSON artifact, health/API endpoints, and this human-readable dashboard.
